🧮 How It Works

📊

Historical Data

900+ FIFA World Cup matches from 1930–2022 used to train team strength ratings

⚡

ELO Ratings

Dynamic ELO system with stage-weighted K-factors, recency weighting (2× for post-2014), and goal-difference bonuses

🎲

Monte Carlo

50,000 full tournament simulations using Poisson goal sampling and Dixon-Coles draw correction

🏟️

2026 Format

48-team expanded format with 12 groups, top 2 + 8 best 3rd-place teams advancing to Round of 32
